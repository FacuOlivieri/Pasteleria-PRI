Four steps

How to order

No apps, no accounts you will never use again. Four steps, and the rest is our job.

  1. 1

    Browse the catalog

    Filter by category or by what you feel like spending. Every piece lists what is inside it, because surprises belong in the box, not in the recipe.

  2. 2

    Fill your cart

    Add what you want and adjust the amounts. Your cart follows you around the site, so you can keep looking without losing anything.

  3. 3

    Confirm your order

    Tell us the date and the address. We confirm by email the same day, and we only start baking once you have said yes to everything.

  4. 4

    We bake, you enjoy

    Your order is made the same morning it reaches you, packed cold and delivered by someone who works here. Never a courier who has no idea what is in the box.
